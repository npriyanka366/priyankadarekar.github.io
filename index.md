## Profile

Dr. Priyanka Darekar is an Associate Professor in the area of **Human Resource Management and General Management** with over **15 years of academic, research, and training experience**.

She holds a **Doctorate in Human Resource Management** from Savitribai Phule Pune University and is an approved **PG Teacher and Research Supervisor (PhD Guide)**. Two doctoral scholars have successfully completed their PhD under her supervision.

Her teaching and research interests span **organizational behavior, strategic management, business research methods, psychology, and HR analytics**.

She has received national recognition including the **Outstanding Faculty in HR Award (Dewang Mehta National Education Awards)** and **Business Leader of the Year – HR (WHRD Congress)**.
